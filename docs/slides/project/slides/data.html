Data

Two real sources with real defects

Check-ups, partner clinic

6 anonymized EHR exports (xlsx), 650–750 rows, 15–28 protocols: labs, ultrasound, echo, endoscopy, 8–12 specialists.

No lab reference ranges; weight 75 vs 95 kg across documents; P005’s export is shifted by one row (Hb “33.3 g/L”).

Inpatient records, another clinic

6 cardiology admissions, 90–139-page PDFs (form 003/u) stitched from many doctors’ documents.

Free text, lab tables with their own ranges, personal data — masked on load; audit: zero residues.

Defects were kept on purpose: tools and models must cope with them and recognize corrupted data.

Both sources map onto one record model with stable ids: P003:r351 for a check-up row, K04:p71:b3 for a PDF block. Answers cite these ids.
