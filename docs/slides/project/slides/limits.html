Limitations and next steps

What this study does not show yet

Limitations

  • 12 records; power only for large effects
  • Questions written by the tool author, not blind
  • Report system tuned on the same 10 summaries
  • Regex scoring: 92 % agreement with a manual review of 24 answers, κ = 0.73; errors lean lenient
  • NHANES ranges: another population and analysers

Next steps

  • Blind question set written with clinicians
  • More records from the partner clinics
  • Guideline rules for audit verdicts, not only facts
  • Trends across repeated check-ups
  • Local model on a GPU server in the clinic
The manual review of the scorer was done by the AI assistant used in the project, not by a clinician: a stratified sample of 24 final-run answers. Both disagreements were the scorer being too lenient (a keyword match without the right fact). The defects found during the study and their effect on earlier numbers are listed openly in the report, section 9.
