Context

A doctor needs a fast, verifiable answer from the patient’s record

AI-mnesis builds modules for clinics: check-up analytics and audit of medical documentation. One record is hundreds of lab and protocol rows, or a 90–140-page PDF assembled by many doctors.

Data stays in Russia

Federal law 152-FZ rules out foreign cloud LLMs. What remains: GigaChat (Sber) or an open-weights model on own hardware.

Verifiability

Every claim must point to a row of the record. No data means saying so, not inventing a value.

Cost and speed

Models allowed under these rules are weaker than the frontier. The architecture has to compensate.

Market constraints set the experiment: the model is either a Russian cloud model or a small local one. The question is how to get accuracy out of them.
