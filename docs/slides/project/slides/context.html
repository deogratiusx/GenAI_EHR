Context

A doctor needs a fast, verifiable answer from the patient’s record

This project is part of AI-mnesis (ai-mnesis.ru), a startup whose mission is to give the doctor’s time back to the patient: up to 70 % of it goes to history taking and paperwork. AI-mnesis takes over medical documentation end to end — patient interviews, record analytics, audit — with a pilot at a regional clinical hospital.

Here we ask one research question from that work: how well can an LLM answer a doctor’s questions over a whole record — hundreds of lab and protocol rows, or a 90–140-page PDF from many doctors?

Data stays in Russia

Federal law 152 rules out foreign cloud LLMs. What remains: GigaChat (Sber) or an open-weights model on own hardware.

Verifiability

Every claim must point to a row of the record. No data means saying so, not inventing a value.

Cost and speed

Models allowed under these rules are weaker than the frontier. The architecture has to compensate.

Market constraints set the experiment: the model is either a Russian cloud model or a small local one. The question is how to get accuracy out of them.
