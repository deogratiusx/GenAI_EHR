What the paired tests say

Paired sign-flip permutation tests, 10 000 permutations; Holm over 29 contrasts. Filled markers: significant after Holm (H1a for all models, Lite − Max); open: not significant.

Tools beat the code interpreter for every model. Against the whole record and map-reduce they are not worse, at a fraction of the tokens. The dossier raises critical recall for all four models, but 9 records per model is too few to survive the correction. Med-Qwen is 0.11 below its base, borderline after Holm (p = 0.065).
